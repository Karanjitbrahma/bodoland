Our Editorial Standards & Code of Ethics

We adhere to the International Fact-Checking Network principles and the Press Council of India code. We hold enterprise reporting in the Northeast to the highest scrutiny.

shield

Public Retraction Guarantee

Factual corrections are published transparently within 24 hours of notification.

money_off

Zero Paid PR Policy

We maintain a strict firewall between editorial coverage and commercial partnerships. No startup, agency, or corporation can purchase profiles, rankings, or interviews in our editorial columns. Sponsored whitepapers and partner events are explicitly branded with mandatory visual disclosure.

fact_check

Fact-Checking & Financial Verification

Every quantitative metric cited—including ARR, gross merchandise value (GMV), customer acquisition cost, and workforce count—must be validated through GST returns, Udyam MSME certifications, or MCA registry filings before publication.

diversity_3

Regional Representation

We mandate that at least 40% of our investigative features spotlight indigenous artisans, women-owned village enterprises, and agri-entrepreneurs working across forest produce, sericulture, and bamboo value chains outside major urban cores.
